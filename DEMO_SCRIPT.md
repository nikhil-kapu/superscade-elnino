# Voiceover script (2:00, timed to `demo-video/superscade-elnino-demo.mp4`)

**[0:00–0:15] The problem (Bay Area 3D, storm starts)**
This winter, El Niño could flood the Bay Area. When it does, the data to respond is siloed: hospitals, shelters, the power grid, road closures and rescue teams each live in a different agency's system. Stitching it together takes rooms full of people on phones and spreadsheets.

**[0:15–0:23] The solution**
SUPERSCADE fixes that. Every district, responder and resource becomes an AI agent with its own durable actor, and they coordinate in real time. Let's hit the Bay with a massive storm.

**[0:23–0:34] Real map**
Creeks flood, hills slide, highways close. This is the real Bay Area: hospitals, emergency operations centers, fire stations, pump stations and substations.

**[0:34–0:46] Agents on Terse**
A chief agent triages the districts at risk and spins up one agent per city, plus helpers and a verifier. Each one is a Terse Durable Actor with its own live memory of what it was told, planned and refused.

**[0:46–0:58] Terse ledger**
Helicopters, rescue teams and shelter beds live in one Terse ledger actor. It handles one claim at a time, so when ten cities ask at once, nothing is double-booked. A denial forces a re-plan.

**[0:58–1:11] Agent Relay**
Instead of phone trees, the agents talk over Agent Relay:
- the chief DMs each district its assignment,
- districts post plans to the storm channel,
- the verifier replies in the thread.

Every agent reads its inbox before it plans.

**[1:11–1:20] Observability**
Every assignment, claim, denial and verdict is recorded and visible live. You can see who was told what, and why a plan was rejected.

**[1:20–1:45] San Francisco in 3D**
Now zoom into San Francisco in 3D. We start the same storm with a king tide, and the same agent team spins up for the city's neighborhoods.

**[1:45–1:55] Impact**
The Embarcadero, Mission Bay and the Marina flood, Ocean Beach erodes, and the response is already being coordinated.

**[1:55–2:00] Close**
SUPERSCADE: rehearse the disaster before it happens. Built on Terse, Agent Relay and OpenAI.
